# Awesome-Video-Game-Compilation

# Top Video Game Compilation Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Recompilation Ports, Decompilation Projects & Unified Game Launchers*  
**Last updated: October 2026**

This repository tracks notable **commercial game compilation releases** and **open-source projects** that achieve the same goal: bringing multiple classic games together under one roof — whether through official remasters, community recompilations, decompilation projects, or unified launchers that consolidate scattered libraries.

**Examples** include Halo: The Master Chief Collection, Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection, Super Mario 3D All-Stars, Mega Man Legacy Collection, BioShock: The Collection, Mass Effect Legendary Edition, Crash Bandicoot N. Sane Trilogy, Spyro Reignited Trilogy, Sonic Origins, and TRX: Tomb Raider I-III Community Edition (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: The open-source community has produced remarkable compilation equivalents. **TRX** unifies Tomb Raider I, II, and III into a single engine with modern enhancements. **Ship of Harkinian** brings Ocarina of Time to PC with mod support. **Playnite** and **Pegasus Frontend** consolidate entire game libraries — including emulated titles — into one unified interface. This section is heavily expanded.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Halo: The Master Chief Collection](https://www.xbox.com/games/halo-the-master-chief-collection)**  
  Microsoft's flagship compilation bundling Halo: Combat Evolved Anniversary, Halo 2: Anniversary, Halo 3, Halo 3: ODST, Halo: Reach, and Halo 4 in one package. Features unified matchmaking, custom game browser, and Forge mode across all titles.

- **[Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection](https://www.playstation.com/games/uncharted-the-nathan-drake-collection/)**  
  Sony's remastered compilation of Uncharted: Drake's Fortune, Uncharted 2: Among Thieves, and Uncharted 3: Drake's Deception for PS4. Enhanced visuals at 1080p/60fps with improved controls.

- **[Super Mario 3D All-Stars](https://www.nintendo.com/store/products/super-mario-3d-all-stars-switch/)**  
  Nintendo's limited-release compilation of Super Mario 64, Super Mario Sunshine, and Super Mario Galaxy for Nintendo Switch. Features updated resolutions and motion controls.

- **[Mega Man Legacy Collection](https://www.capcom.com/megaman/)**  
  Capcom's compilation of Mega Man 1-6 with added Museum Mode, challenge remixes, and rewind functionality. Spawned multiple sequels (Legacy Collection 2, X Legacy Collections).

- **[BioShock: The Collection](https://www.2k.com/bioshock/)**  
  2K's remastered compilation of BioShock, BioShock 2, and BioShock Infinite with all single-player DLC. Includes director's commentary and concept art galleries.

- **[Mass Effect Legendary Edition](https://www.ea.com/games/mass-effect/mass-effect-legendary-edition)**  
  EA/BioWare's comprehensive remaster of Mass Effect, Mass Effect 2, and Mass Effect 3 with all DLC, unified character creator, and improved combat mechanics.

- **[Crash Bandicoot N. Sane Trilogy](https://www.crashbandicoot.com/)**  
  Activision's ground-up remake of Crash Bandicoot, Cortex Strikes Back, and Warped with modern graphics while preserving original level design.

- **[Spyro Reignited Trilogy](https://www.spyrothedragon.com/)**  
  Activision's remake of Spyro the Dragon, Ripto's Rage!, and Year of the Dragon with rebuilt visuals and remastered soundtrack.

- **[Sonic Origins](https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_Origins)**  
  Sega's compilation of Sonic the Hedgehog, Sonic 2, Sonic 3 & Knuckles, and Sonic CD with widescreen support, Anniversary Mode, and new animations.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[TRX – Tomb Raider I-III: Community Edition](https://github.com/LostArtefacts/TRX)**  
  **The leading open-source game compilation**, unifying Tomb Raider 1, 2, and 3 into a single engine through decompilation . GPL-3.0 licensed. Features restored braid in TR1, enemy health bar and UI scaling, photo mode, 3D pickups, skybox support, updated moveset including sprint, customizable draw distance, developer console, detailed level stats, and PS1 UI options . **All three games fully playable from beginning to end** with Windows, Linux, and macOS support . Full controller support with customizable layouts . **The de facto model for open-source compilation projects.**

- **[Ship of Harkinian](https://github.com/HarbourMasters/Shipwright)**  
  **Native PC port of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time** through the OOT decompilation project. Supports mods, HD textures, widescreen, high frame rates, and extensive configuration options. Part of the HarbourMasters ecosystem that includes **2 Ship 2 Harkinian** (Majora's Mask) and **Lighthouse** (Banjo-Kazooie) . **The flagship example of recompilation bringing N64 classics to PC.**

- **[SpaghettiKart](https://github.com/HarbourMasters/SpaghettiKart)**  
  **Native PC port of Mario Kart 64** with visual enhancements, widescreen support, and modern resolutions . Part of the growing HarbourMasters family of N64 recompilation ports.

- **[GoldenEye 007 Recompiled](https://github.com/GoldenEye-Recomp/GoldenEye-Recomp)**  
  **Native PC port of GoldenEye 007** through the N64 decompilation project. Supports modern resolutions, high frame rates, and mouse/keyboard controls .

- **[Perfect Dark PC Port](https://github.com/fgsfdsfgs/perfect_dark)**  
  Native PC port of Perfect Dark through decompilation, with support for modern resolutions and high frame rates .

- **[Unleashed Recomp](https://github.com/hedge-dev/UnleashedRecomp)**  
  **Native PC recompilation of Sonic Unleashed** (Xbox 360/PS3) with improved performance and modern resolutions .

- **[Playnite](https://github.com/JosefNemec/Playnite)**  
  **The leading open-source game library manager and launcher**, MIT licensed . Unifies Steam, Epic, GOG, EA App, Battle.net, Ubisoft Connect, and other services into one interface . **Includes full emulation support** — add ROMs and console games alongside PC titles . Fullscreen mode with controller support, IGDB metadata integration, playtime tracking, community themes, and extensions . Windows-focused with Linux support planned for 2026 . **The de facto open-source compilation launcher for managing scattered libraries.**

- **[Pegasus Frontend](https://github.com/mmatyas/pegasus-frontend)**  
  **Cross-platform graphical frontend for browsing and launching game libraries** with high customizability and performance . Runs on Windows, Linux, Mac, Android, and Raspberry Pi . Full control over UI through themes — can look like Kodi, Steam, or any custom design . **EmulationStation backward compatibility** for existing gamelist files . Gamepad support and portable mode . **The best choice for console-style living room setups.**

- **[EmuHub](https://github.com/Tw1tchy0/EmuHub)**  
  **Portable Windows game launcher unifying 16 consoles** into one interface with automatic box art fetching via SteamGridDB . Supports Nintendo Switch, 3DS, DS, GBA, GBC, N64, GameCube, Wii, NES, SNES, Genesis, PlayStation, PS2, PS3, PSP, and Xbox 360 . **One application to browse, search, and launch your entire ROM library across all consoles** . No emulators or ROMs included — point to your own installations .

- **[RetroHub](https://github.com/retrohub-org/retrohub)**  
  **Retro-gaming library frontend** designed to be user-friendly and highly customizable . Leverages Godot engine for themes, enabling versatile 2D and 3D interfaces . Pre-configured with a large number of systems and emulators, requiring next to zero configuration . MIT licensed source code .

- **[iiSU](https://github.com/iiSU-emulation/iiSU)**  
  **Android emulation frontend** inspired by Nintendo DS dual-screen interface . Aggregates games from multiple emulators into a unified, controller-friendly interface . Features customizable home screen grids, animated widgets for favorites, list view (XMB-style), Discord integration for multiplayer coordination, and **dual-screen handheld support** (AYANEO Flip 1S, Pocket DS) . Alpha stage but actively developed .

- **[Crispy Trilogy](https://github.com/nicoku007/CRISPY-TRILOGY-for-Heretic-hexen-Strife)**  
  **Unified launcher for Crispy Heretic, Crispy Hexen, and Crispy Strife** — three classic Doom-engine games . C# Windows Forms frontend with independent configurations per game session, multiplayer settings, and individual WAD browsers . **Acts as a trilogy collection launcher** for these classic titles . Compatible with the "Faith Renewed" expansion for Heretic .

- **[JàmmBox](https://github.com/KerHack-Libre/JammBox)**  
  **DOS game compilation with unified launcher** via DOSBox . Pre-configured classic DOS games (Prince of Persia, Prince of Persia 2, Ms. Pac-Man: Maze Madness) with C-based menu system . Designed for simple, direct retro-gaming without complex configuration . Includes libzip for game archives and ncurses for the menu interface .

- **[Omakade](https://github.com/btsouth/omakade)**  
  **Unified launcher for local and linked game libraries** including RetroArch playlists, Battle.net games, Steam non-Steam shortcuts, and manual native games . Features saved filters as dynamic queries, batch organization, backup/restore, and Couch Mode for controller navigation . Supports artwork, tags, collections, and launch history .

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **EmulationStation** — Classic open-source frontend for organizing and launching emulated games with gamelist.xml metadata.
- **LaunchBox** — Free tier available for Windows library management with Big Box mode for living room setups .
- **Steam ROM Manager** — Open-source tool for adding ROMs and emulated games to Steam as non-Steam shortcuts.
- **EmuDeck** — Open-source script for Steam Deck that installs and configures emulators, ROM managers, and frontends with unified Steam integration.
- **Batocera** — Open-source retro-gaming distribution that turns any PC into a dedicated console with unified emulation frontend.

**Frameworks for building custom compilation solutions**: Combine **TRX** as the reference model for decompilation-based compilations with modern enhancements . Use **Playnite** for unifying PC store libraries with emulation support . Deploy **Pegasus Frontend** or **RetroHub** for console-style living room interfaces . Leverage **EmuHub** for portable ROM library management across 16 consoles . For Android handhelds, **iiSU** provides dual-screen-aware emulation frontend . Note that true commercial compilations with ground-up remakes, unified multiplayer, and cross-title progression remain primarily proprietary territory; open-source projects provide strong recompilation ports, unified launchers, and library management foundations that require individual game ownership.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- **Recompilation and decompilation projects require original game files**. They do not include ROMs or game assets — you must own a legal copy of each title .
- Open-source compilation projects vary significantly in maturity. **TRX is fully playable**, while others may be early-stage or platform-specific .
- **Emulation legality varies by jurisdiction** — ROM downloads of games you don't own may be illegal. Always use your own legally obtained game files.
- The open-source ecosystem provides strong recompilation ports, unified launchers, and library management foundations, but **ground-up remakes and unified multiplayer across titles** remain primarily commercial offerings.
